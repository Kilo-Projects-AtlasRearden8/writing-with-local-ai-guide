# Writing with Local AI Models via Ollama

A beginner-friendly manual and static site for writing with AI models that run entirely on your own computer. Drafting, editing, brainstorming, and privacy — step by step, in plain English, with nothing ever leaving your computer.

## Who this is for

This guide was written for anyone who wants to write with AI without sending their words to a cloud service: authors, nurses, students, small-business owners, and anyone curious about local AI. No coding required.

It is published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**, an Indiana LLC focused on beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools. Learn more at [dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

## What's inside

- `docs/01-setup-ollama.md` — Install Ollama and run your first model, step by step
- `docs/02-model-choices.md` — Picking a local model for writing (size, speed, quality)
- `docs/03-drafting.md` — Using a local model to draft stories, articles, and letters
- `docs/04-editing.md` — Editing and polishing your own writing with local AI
- `docs/05-brainstorming.md` — Brainstorming titles, plots, and ideas locally
- `docs/06-privacy.md` — Why nothing leaves your computer, and what that means
- `docs/07-prompt-templates.md` — Printable prompt templates you can fill in by hand
- `site/` — A static HTML version of this manual, ready to open in any browser

## How to use this manual

1. Read `docs/01-setup-ollama.md` to install Ollama and pull a writing model.
2. Work through the drafting, editing, and brainstorming guides in order.
3. Print `docs/07-prompt-templates.md` (or open `site/templates.html`) and keep the templates beside your keyboard.
4. Browse the same content as a website: open `site/index.html` in your browser. No server required.

## Privacy promise

Every model mentioned in this guide runs locally through Ollama. Your writing, your prompts, and your data stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and no API key is required.
