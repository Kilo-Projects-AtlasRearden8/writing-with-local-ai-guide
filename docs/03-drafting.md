# 3. Drafting with Local AI

Drafting means getting words onto the page when a blank screen feels
intimidating. A local model can give you a starting point — nothing more,
nothing less. You are still the author; the model is just a very patient writing
partner who never gets tired.

## How to use a model for drafting

Open a chat with your chosen model:

```bash
ollama run llama3.2:3b
```

Then paste a prompt like one below, replacing the bracketed parts with your own
ideas.

### Draft a short story opening

```text
Write the opening scene of a cozy mystery set in a small-town library.
The protagonist is a retired nurse named Mara who loves chess.
Tone: gentle, curious, slightly humorous. About 300 words.
```

### Draft an article or blog post

```text
Write a 500-word beginner-friendly article about starting a vegetable garden in Indiana.
Include: why fall is a good time to start, three easy crops, and one encouragement for first-time gardeners.
Tone: warm and encouraging, like a friend explaining it over coffee.
```

### Draft a letter or email

```text
Write a polite but firm email to a landlord requesting a repair for a leaking kitchen faucet.
Mention that it has been dripping for two weeks. Keep it under 150 words.
```

## Tips for better drafts

- **Be specific about tone.** Words like "warm," "professional," "humorous," or "gentle" change the output a lot.
- **Set a length.** "About 200 words" or "three short paragraphs" keeps the draft focused.
- **Give the model a role.** "You are a kind editor" or "You write cozy mysteries" helps it stay in character.
- **Draft in pieces.** Ask for one scene or section at a time rather than a whole book.

## Remember: you are in charge

A local model's first draft is a starting point, not a finished piece. Feel free
to:

- Throw the whole thing away and write your own
- Keep one sentence and rewrite the rest
- Ask the model to "try again, but funnier" or "make it shorter"

Because the model runs on your computer, you can ask it to rewrite as many times
as you like — no one is watching, and nothing is being saved to a cloud account.
