Write with AI — without sending your words anywhere

This manual walks you through writing with AI models that run on your own computer using Ollama. No account, no API key, no cloud. Your drafts, prompts, and ideas stay on your machine.

Nothing leaves your computer.

Privacy promise. Every model in this guide runs locally through Ollama. Your writing never leaves your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and no API key is required. Works offline once a model is downloaded.

How to use this site

  1. Install Ollama and pull a writing model (we suggest llama3.2:3b to start).
  2. Work through Drafting, Editing, and Brainstorming in order.
  3. Print the prompt templates and keep them beside your keyboard.
  4. Prefer Markdown? Read the same content in docs/.
