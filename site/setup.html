What Ollama is

Ollama is a free program that runs AI models locally on your own computer. Think of it like a music player for AI: Ollama downloads and runs models, and your writing never leaves your machine.

Step 1: Download Ollama

  1. Go to ollama.com.
  2. Click Download and choose your computer: Mac, Windows, or Linux.
  3. Install Ollama like any other app — open the installer and follow the prompts.

Once installed, Ollama runs in the background. You do not need to keep the Ollama website open.

Step 2: Open your terminal

Ollama is controlled from your computer's command line:

  • Mac: press Command + Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  • Windows: press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter.
  • Linux: open your terminal application.

Step 3: Check that Ollama is working

ollama --version

You should see something like:

ollama version is 0.5.7

If you see ollama: command not found, Ollama may not be installed or your terminal needs restarting. Restart your terminal and try again.

Step 4: Pull your first writing model

Models are the brains Ollama runs. To download a small, fast model good for writing:

ollama pull llama3.2:3b

This downloads the model to your computer. It may take a few minutes. Once downloaded, it stays on your computer — you do not need internet to use it afterward.

Other good first models:

ollama pull phi3:mini
ollama pull mistral-nemo:12b

Step 5: Run your first prompt

ollama run llama3.2:3b

Type something like:

Write a short, friendly opening paragraph for a letter to a new neighbor.

The model will type out a reply on your screen, entirely on your computer. Type /bye to exit the chat.

Step 6: Try a one-shot prompt

You do not need to start a chat. Run a single prompt and exit:

ollama run llama3.2:3b "Write a three-sentence bedtime story about a curious raccoon."

What just happened?

  • Ollama downloaded a model file to your computer (usually a few gigabytes).
  • When you run a prompt, your computer does the thinking — no server involved.
  • Your words never leave your machine. No account, no API key.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
ollama: command not foundReinstall Ollama, then restart your terminal
Pull takes a long timeThe model is a few gigabytes; leave your computer awake and on Wi-Fi until it finishes
Model replies slowlyYour computer is doing all the work. Try a smaller model (llama3.2:1b or phi3:mini)
"Not enough memory"Close other apps and try a smaller model
